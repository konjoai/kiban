## Decision

**Pass.** This sprint is not a replacement exercise -- the budget has room for the full
candidate set with a comfortable margin, so Phase 2 (had it shipped) would not need to
displace anything to make room. See `KT-13.1.md` for why Phase 2 does not ship this
sprint regardless: KT-13.2 passing is necessary but not sufficient -- an invariant still
has to earn its place by measured defect reduction (KT-13.1's job), not merely by
fitting in the budget.
